# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists

The Green Office Labels is a programme enabling student organisations at the University of Groningen to assess and improve their sustainability practices. Organizations complete a questionnaire and receive sustainability certifications based on their responses.

This project exists to provide organisations with a digital platform for achieving sustainability certifications through structured self-assessment and to provide the Green Office with a platfrom for the necessary verification processes. The programme currently uses Google Forms, but this is tedious and not flexible enough to expand the programme, so this project replaces the current method in favour of a well-rouned web-app.

## Problems Solved

- Lack of flexibility of the current Google-Forms-based approach
- Tedious manual certification processes
- Non user-friendly application process for organisations

## How It Should Work

1. Organizations complete digital questionnaires about their practices, providing evidence where needed
2. System automatically scores responses against sustainability criteria
3. The Green Office manually verifies the provided evidence and scores responses that can't automatically be graded (e.g. open ended)
4. Certifications based on the ratio of score to obtainable score are awarded. These use the Leaf Score, which goes from 0.5 to 5 leaves with 0.5 leaf increments
5. Analytics dashboard is available for visualising how well organisations do
6. Public page is available for transparent results of all organisations with a leaderboard
